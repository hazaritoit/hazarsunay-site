Building Bridges: Global Changemakers at Summer Academy 2025

Last week, I participated in the Summer Academy 2025 at Karlshochschule International University in Karlsruhe. The program, Building Bridges: Future Skills for Global Changemakers, was organised by InterCultur gGmbH with the generous support of Stiftung Mercator. The academy brought together young participants from diverse countries and backgrounds for nine days of intensive learning, collaboration, and reflection on global challenges.

The academic program offered a comprehensive set of perspectives on international relations and contemporary global issues. Highlights included lectures on Realism, Constructivism, and Feminism in International Relations by Anthony Teitler, PhD, in-depth discussions on conflict resolution and democratic futures with Marcel Erik Lemmer, and a critical workshop on global security led by Dr. Ines Kappert. The sessions on leadership and personal development by Robert Paschmann, as well as intercultural collaboration workshops facilitated by Rebeca Schröder Crespillo, contributed significantly to the program’s holistic approach.

Participants also explored themes of sustainability and innovation during a visit to the Institute for Technology Assessment and Systems Analysis (ITAS). Cultural excursions to the ZKM Center for Art and Media and Heidelberg Castle offered opportunities to connect theoretical debates with cultural and historical contexts.

We concluded the academy with a visit from Oleksandra Gnyp of Stiftung Mercator GmbH, who presented the foundation’s broader vision and encouraged us to remain engaged through the MercatorGlobe community.

I would also like to highlight the creative work produced during the program. The outcomes of this year’s Summer Academy were brought to life through the artistry and intellectual creativity of Maria Karipidou, the mind behind thinkanddraw.de. Her work captured the spirit of our collective learning experience and added a meaningful visual dimension to the program.

My sincere thanks go to Amandeep Saini-Egbers, Laura Martella, and Farisha Thakur for their continuous support throughout the academy, as well as to Stefanie Vogler-Lipp, whose efforts behind the scenes made the program possible.

The Summer Academy was an inspiring and intellectually stimulating experience. I left Karlsruhe with new perspectives, strong connections, and a renewed motivation to contribute to global dialogue and positive social change.

IV

Research

My research looks at how states and societies use culture, education and communication abroad: from heritage diplomacy in the Balkans to diaspora identity in Germany and digital diplomacy on social media. The papers below were written during my studies and are unpublished academic papers.

Master's Thesis In progress · University of Siena

Germany as a Model in European Migration and Integration Debates: A Diachronic Analysis from the 1970s to the Post-2015 Refugee Crisis

Unpublished academic paperCultural Diplomacy

TİKA as an Instrument of Türkiye's Cultural Diplomacy in Bosnia: A Remote Sensing Analysis of the Reconstruction of Ferhadija Mosque in Banja Luka

An analysis of Türkiye's cultural diplomacy through TİKA's reconstruction efforts in Bosnia, supported by satellite imagery to examine heritage restoration and soft power.

Unpublished academic paperHeritage & Memory

From Prizren to Istanbul: Cultural Memory and Heritage of Kosovo Turks in Türkiye

A study of cultural memory, identity transmission, and heritage practices among Kosovo Turks who migrated to Türkiye.

Unpublished academic paperDiaspora & Identity

Diyanet's Role in Preserving Turkish Identity within the Turkish-German Diaspora

An evaluation of how Diyanet shapes cultural identity and belonging among Turkish communities in Germany.

Unpublished academic paperDigital Diplomacy

U.S. Embassy Türkiye on X: Digital Diplomacy

An analysis of the U.S. Embassy's communication strategies on X, focusing on digital diplomacy, public engagement, and narrative framing.

Unpublished academic paperInternational Education

Higher Education as Export: The Political Economy of International Students in Türkiye

An examination of Türkiye's internationalization of higher education and the political economy behind international student recruitment.

Unpublished academic paperComparative Politics

Populism and Democratic Backsliding: A Comparative Analysis of Türkiye and Hungary

A comparative political analysis evaluating the mechanisms of democratic erosion in Türkiye and Hungary through populist governance.

Unpublished academic paperSustainability

Reflective Report on SDG 12 – iWARM Model Waste Calculator

A reflective assessment of sustainable consumption patterns using the iWARM Model, focusing on personal environmental impact and SDG 12.
